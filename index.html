Programmer.

I’m a Senior AI Programmer with 8 years of professional experience in game development, working on AI systems based on Utility AI, Behaviour Trees, Finite State Machines and GOAP, among others.

I have implemented systems using both ECS and OOP. Having worked on both Unity and Unreal Engine (with a special focus on GAS), I have a proven track record of how my understanding of both engines has benefitted my work. I am constantly striving to enrich my knowledge of Unreal Engine 5, focusing on GAS, AI framework (BTs, Smart Objects, EQS, Perception) and debugging tools.

With a background in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, my academic experience ranges from traditional knowledge-based techniques, to modern reinforcement learning and evolutionary algorithms, in addition to a solid software engineering skill set.

Supported by a strong design understanding, I am driven by my desire to build player-first experiences, in which AI plays a fundamental role towards immersion.

NPCs that Feel Alive: Achieving Variety and Complexity with Limited Resources

Game AI Uncovered Vol. 4, CRC Press, Feb 4 2026

An approach to implementing an AI-rich interactive village, in which each NPC plays a specific role towards making the environment feel alive. It discusses the technical implementation of each NPC role, reviewing the use of Behaviour Trees, Finite State Machines and Utility AI, while taking into account resources available and intended behavioural complexity of every agent.
